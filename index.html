    Zijun Jiao

    Research Assistant, The Future Laboratory, Tsinghua University

    I work on data-driven optimization and decision-making for sustainable operations: forecasting demand and equipment condition, building predictive models that stay reliable under limited compute and noisy inputs, and connecting those predictions to the maintenance, capacity and service decisions they are meant to inform.

    Since June 2025 I have been a research assistant at The Future Laboratory, Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Haipeng Mi, where I build the backend and tool-agent orchestration for an air-gapped language-model system used by a heavy-haul railway operator. Before that I completed an MSc in Data Science for Sustainability at the National University of Singapore, and a BSc in Mathematical Sciences at the University of Southampton, where my dissertation compared neural-network and tensor-network representations of quantum many-body ground states.

    I am applying for PhD programmes in operations research and industrial engineering starting in Fall 2027.
