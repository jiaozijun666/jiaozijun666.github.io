LLM-Based Business Support System for Heavy-Haul Railways

Developed a secure, on-premise LLM-based business support system for heavy-haul railway operations, designed to support enterprise knowledge services, intelligent document processing, multimodal interaction, and domain-specific decision support. The project integrates railway-domain language models, retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge graphs, multimodal AI, and workflow orchestration within a unified local deployment architecture.

The system follows a layered design spanning enterprise data and knowledge, domain-adapted LLM services, hybrid retrieval and reasoning, multimodal perception, and application-level workflow orchestration. A private deployment architecture was adopted to keep sensitive railway documents, meeting recordings, and operational data within the enterprise network while supporting scalable model inference and knowledge services.

Core Technical Components

Domain-Adapted LLM: Built a railway-specific language model pipeline using domain adaptation and parameter-efficient fine-tuning, enabling the model to better understand railway terminology, business processes, safety regulations, and enterprise writing styles.

Hybrid RAG and Knowledge Reasoning: Combined vector retrieval and keyword retrieval with reranking to improve knowledge recall for both semantic questions and exact railway terminology. The system further integrates a knowledge graph with retrieval-augmented generation to support structured reasoning, evidence tracing, and more reliable answers for complex operational queries.

Multimodal Processing: Integrated speech and document understanding capabilities through Whisper-based automatic speech recognition and PaddleOCR-based document parsing. Meeting recordings, scanned documents, PDFs, and operational materials can be converted into structured text and further processed by the LLM and knowledge-retrieval pipeline.

Security and Traceability: Designed fine-grained access control, local data processing, knowledge-level permission filtering, and audit logging to support secure use of enterprise information. Generated answers are linked to source knowledge whenever possible, improving traceability and reliability in safety-critical scenarios.

Featured Component: Intelligent Meeting Minutes Generation

Designed an end-to-end pipeline that transforms long railway meeting recordings into structured, actionable meeting minutes. The workflow combines audio preprocessing, domain-adapted speech recognition, speaker diarization, hierarchical LLM summarization, structured information extraction, and railway-specific safety checks.

Raw meeting audio is first standardized and denoised, followed by voice activity detection and railway-adapted Whisper speech recognition. The system then applies speaker diarization to identify different participants and align speaker labels with timestamped transcripts. To process long meetings efficiently, the transcript is divided into semantic topics, summarized at the segment level, and subsequently fused into a higher-level meeting summary.

The final LLM stage extracts structured information including meeting topics, key discussions, decisions, unresolved issues, action items, responsible persons, and deadlines. A railway-specific safety module additionally extracts safety-related discussions and checks generated content against relevant safety requirements before producing the final minutes.

Evaluation

The meeting-minutes pipeline was evaluated on real railway meetings covering operational handover, safety analysis, and technical discussions.

WER: 8.1%   ·   DER: 9.9%   ·   Action-item Precision: 89%   ·   Action-item Recall: 83%   ·   Action-item F1: 85.9%

Expert Evaluation: 4.3 / 5   ·   Information Completeness: 4.5 / 5   ·   Processing Efficiency: approximately 2.2 minutes for a 1-hour meeting

Applications

The platform supports a range of enterprise scenarios, including knowledge Q&A, intelligent writing, PPT generation, meeting-minute generation, document processing, Excel data analysis, procurement support, and knowledge management. These applications share a common model and knowledge-service layer, allowing the system to reuse domain knowledge across different business workflows.
